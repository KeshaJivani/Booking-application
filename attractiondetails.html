About This Attraction

The Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum on the south bank of the Yamuna river in a vast Mughal garden that encompasses nearly 17 hectares, in the Agra District in Uttar Pradesh. Built in 1632, in the memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan, it is widely recognized as one of the most exquisite examples of Mughal architecture. It is also considered one of the greatest works of art in the world.

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Taj Mahal attracts millions of visitors from around the world every year. Its stunning white marble construction, intricate inlay work, and symmetrical gardens make it one of the most photographed monuments in the world. The best time to visit is during the cooler months from October to March.
