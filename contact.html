Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my booking? ▼
You can cancel your booking through your account dashboard or by contacting our support team. Refunds depend on the cancellation policy of your chosen property.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and digital wallets. All payments are secured with SSL encryption.
How can I modify my reservation? ▼
You can modify dates and guests through your account. Contact support if you need to change the property or make other changes.
Is breakfast included in the booking? ▼
It depends on the room option you selected. Check your booking confirmation email for full details about what's included.
Can I request early check-in? ▼
Yes, you can request early check-in during the booking process or by contacting the property directly. Subject to availability.
