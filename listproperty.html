Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I earn?

Your earnings depend on your property's location, season, and pricing strategy. Many hosts earn thousands per month.

What if I get a booking?

You'll receive a notification immediately. You can accept or decline based on your availability and preferences.

How do I set prices?

You have full control over pricing. You can set different rates for different seasons and adjust them anytime.

What about taxes?

We handle payment processing, but you're responsible for any local taxes on earnings. Consult with a tax professional.

Can I list multiple properties?

Yes! Many hosts manage multiple properties through a single BookMyStay account.

What commission does BookMyStay take?

We charge a standard service fee of 15% on each booking. This includes support and marketing.
